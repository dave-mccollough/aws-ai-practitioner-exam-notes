# Amazon Rekognition

- Find objects, people, text, scenes in images and videos using ML
- Facial analysis and facial search to do user verificatoin, people counting
- Create a database of 'familiar faces' or compare against celebrities
- Use Cases
  - Labeling
  - Content Moderation
  - Text Detection
  - Face Detection and Analysis
  - Face search and verficiation
  - Celebrity Recognition
  - Pathing (sports game analytics)

- Custom Labels
  - Find logos in social media posts
    - NFL uses it to find their logos
  - Identify products on stores shelves
  - Label your training images and upload them to Amazon Rekognition
  - Only requires a few hunderd images
  - Amazon Rekognition creates a custom model on your image set
  - New subsequent images will be catagroized the custom way you have defined

- Content Moderation
  - Automatically detect inappropate, unwanted, or offensive content
  - Bring down human review to 1-5% of total content volume
  - Integrated with Amazon Augmented AI for human review
  - Custom Moderation Adaptors
    - Extends Rekognition capabilites by providing your own labeled set of images
    - Enhances the accuracy of content moderation or creates specifc use cases of moderation
